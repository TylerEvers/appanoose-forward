What would Public Measure AM change?

The ballot asks voters in unincorporated Appanoose County to repeal the existing 7% hotel/motel tax and replace it with another 7% tax. The tax rate would remain 7%; the proposed change concerns who administers the revenue.

According to Appanoose Forward's understanding of the proposed funding arrangement, administration of the county's hotel/motel tax revenue would return to the Appanoose County Board of Supervisors if the measure passes.

Tourism grants, marketing, events, and initiatives have supported Centerville, Moravia, Moulton, Rathbun, and other parts of the county, including historic preservation, parks and recreation, downtown revitalization, community events, and destination marketing.

Tourism's economic impact

Visitors spent an estimated $37.3 million in Appanoose County in 2025. Tourism spending reaches restaurants, lodging properties, local retailers, attractions, and other businesses. It also contributes to employment and generates public tax revenue.

About Appanoose Forward

Our committee currently opposes Public Measure AM and supports retaining the existing Tourism Commission funding arrangement. We engage with issues affecting the long-term success of Appanoose County and encourage community involvement in public decisions.
